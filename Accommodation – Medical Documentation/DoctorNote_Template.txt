Physician's Note — Workplace Accommodation (OHRC)
Ready-to-sign version for Dr. K. Sotoudeh — Bradford Health Care Centre.

What this note must do (so the doctor doesn't need to know OHRC):
  1. Confirm a disability exists.
  2. State the functional limitations (commuting = trigger / relapse risk).
  3. State the accommodation needed (remote work, as functional limitations require).
  4. State duration (ongoing, with reassessment).
Avoid: treatment details, medication, history, diagnosis — HR only needs
functional information. This is the OHRC chain: disability → limitations →
accommodation → duration. (The employer may generally only ask: does a
disability exist; what are the limitations; can essential duties be performed
with/without accommodation; what accommodation is needed — and must pay for
required medical documentation.)

Peter's report will recommend this note; Dr. Sotoudeh adapts and signs it.

Mr. Wood has a diagnosed medical condition that constitutes a disability
within the meaning of the Ontario Human Rights Code.
Mr. Wood experiences ongoing functional limitations that are triggered and
worsened by commuting to and from the workplace:
- Commuting is a trigger that increases the risk of relapse of his condition.
- Driving causes significant anticipatory anxiety and a heightened stress
  response that persists after arrival and for one to two days afterward.
- Sleep is disrupted on nights before in-office attendance, reducing his
  ability to maintain stability.
- Commuting-related fatigue reduces concentration, consistency, and the
  ability to maintain the routines that support his health.
- These limitations fluctuate; their that support [name]'s health.
- These limitations fluctuate; the impact is greater during periods of
  elevated symptoms.

Recommended accommodation:
Mr. Wood requires a workplace accommodation permitting him to work from home
as his functional limitations require, including full-time remote work during
periods of elevated symptoms. In-office attendance should be limited to the
extent necessary to avoid triggering or worsening these limitations.

[Alternative wording, only if a fixed limit is required:]

per week, with the balance of his work performed remotely.

Duration: Ongoing, with reassessment after 12 months.
