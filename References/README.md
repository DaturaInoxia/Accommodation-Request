# References — OHRC & Ontario Human Rights Code

Authoritative primary sources for the accommodation case. Downloaded 2026-09-29.

## Files

1. **Ontario Human Rights Code (RSO 1990, c. H.19).doc**
   - Source: e-Laws — https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90h19
   - Official Word download (consolidated to July 1, 2025).

2. **OHRC - Policy on ableism and discrimination based on disability (2016).pdf**
   - Source: https://www.ohrc.on.ca/en/policy-ableism-and-discrimination-based-disability

3. **OHRC - Policy on preventing discrimination based on mental health disabilities and addictions.pdf**
   - Source: https://www.ohrc.on.ca/en/policy-preventing-discrimination-based-mental-health-disabilities-and-addictions
   - Most directly relevant to the addiction / episodic-disability framing of this case.

4. **OHRC - Policy and guidelines on disability and the duty to accommodate.pdf**
   - Source: https://www.ohrc.on.ca/en/policy-and-guidelines-disability-and-duty-accommodate

## Key Code provisions to cite (verify exact text in the .doc)

- **s. 1** — right to equal treatment in services, goods and facilities, without discrimination (incl. disability).
- **s. 5** — right to equal treatment in employment.
- **s. 10** — definitions, including "disability".
- **s. 11** — constructive discrimination.
- **s. 17** — disability: essential duties and the undue-hardship standard for accommodation.
